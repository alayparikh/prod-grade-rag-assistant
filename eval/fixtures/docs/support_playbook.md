# Support Playbook: Order Issues

## Late orders

When an order passes its promised date without being delivered, support emails the customer the same day with the current status and a new estimate. For Platinum-tier accounts the account manager also calls.

## Held orders

Look up the order's hold_reason and follow the matching section of the shipping policy. Never promise a new delivery date until the hold is cleared; a hold on credit_check or export_license is outside support's control.

## Damaged on arrival

Ask for photos within 48 hours of delivery and open a carrier claim. Damaged units are replaced from stock ahead of the claim being settled.

## Escalation

Escalate to the logistics lead if an order is more than 5 business days late, if a customer reports a safety incident, or if a Platinum-tier account asks for escalation. The logistics lead responds within 4 business hours.
