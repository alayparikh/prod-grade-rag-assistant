# Shipping Policy

## Carriers and transit times

Orders in the West and Central regions ship with FreightLine; East region orders ship with Coastal Haul; EU orders ship with EuroRoute. Standard transit time after shipping is 3 business days in the West and Central regions, 4 in the East and 7 to the EU.

## Promised delivery date

The promised delivery date (promised_by) is set when the order is placed: the model's lead time plus the region's transit time. An order that is not delivered by its promised date is late, and support contacts the customer proactively.

## Hold reasons

When an order is put on hold, it gets exactly one hold_reason. The promised date is not moved automatically.

### address_verification

The delivery address could not be confirmed by the carrier, usually because the site has a new loading dock or a missing unit number. Support asks the customer to confirm the address and a site contact; the hold is normally cleared within 2 business days of their reply.

### credit_check

The order exceeds the customer's approved credit limit. Finance reviews the account within 3 business days; the customer can clear the hold immediately by paying a 30% deposit.

### export_license

EU orders of the PR-900 need an export licence because of its battery capacity. Licences take 5 to 10 business days to issue, and the order ships as soon as it is granted.

### site_readiness

The customer has told us their site is not ready to receive robots, for example because charging docks are still being installed. The order ships once the customer confirms a delivery window.

### payment_failed

The card or bank transfer for the order was declined. The order is held for up to 7 days for the customer to update payment, then cancelled.
