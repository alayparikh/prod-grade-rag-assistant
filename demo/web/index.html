Demo: search Acme Robotics' documents

DEMO

A working demo of an assistant for a company's documents and orders. Search the files, ask about orders in plain English, or ask the agent to do both. It runs in your browser: no sign-up, no AI service.

  1. Step 1 of 5 · The corpus

    55 passages, 18 files

    Product and order docs, shipping and support policies, engineering runbooks and five resumes, cut at their headings so every passage knows which section it came from. Beside them: an orders database of 80 orders.

  2. Step 2 of 5 · Two searches

    Meaning and exact words

    One search matches what your question means, the other matches the words in it. Their rankings are merged into one candidate list.

  3. Step 3 of 5 · Rerank

    A second read

    A second model reads each candidate beside your question and scores it. The top three are shown. If none scores high enough, the answer is that the documents don't say.

  4. Step 4 of 5 · Orders

    Or straight to the database

    Questions about orders become a SQL query instead. One row answers it, and the policy that explains it comes from the documents.

  5. Step 5 of 5 · The agent

    Both, in one conversation

    Some questions need the query and the document. The agent runs each step in turn, shows what it read, then answers. Ask it from the button at the bottom right.
