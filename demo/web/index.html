Demo: search Acme Robotics' documents

DEMO

Search a robotics company's internal documents. Every step runs in your browser.

Scroll to watch a search
  1. Step 1 of 3 · The corpus

    27 passages, 13 files

    A handbook, two runbooks, policies and five resumes, cut at their headings so every passage knows which section it came from.

  2. Step 2 of 3 · Two searches

    Meaning and exact words

    One search matches what your question means, the other matches the words in it. Their rankings are merged into one candidate list.

  3. Step 3 of 3 · Rerank

    A second read

    A second model reads each candidate beside your question and scores it. The top three are shown. If none scores high enough, the answer is that the documents don't say.

Ask the documents

Answers are passages quoted from the documents, not generated text. No AI service is called, and nothing you type leaves this page.

    Acme Robotics and everyone in these documents are fictional. Search runs on two small open models (all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2), downloaded once — about 50 MB — and cached by your browser.
