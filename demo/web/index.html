Search a robotics company's documents and ask about its orders in plain English. Every step runs in your browser.

Scroll to watch a search
  1. Step 1 of 3 · The corpus

    27 passages, 13 files

    A handbook, two runbooks, policies and five resumes, cut at their headings so every passage knows which section it came from.

  2. Step 2 of 3 · Two searches

    Meaning and exact words

    One search matches what your question means, the other matches the words in it. Their rankings are merged into one candidate list.

  3. Step 3 of 3 · Rerank

    A second read

    A second model reads each candidate beside your question and scores it. The top three are shown. If none scores high enough, the answer is that the documents don't say.

    Ask the orders

    Questions about orders become a SQL query against Acme's orders database, shown before it runs. This demo picks from nine query types by meaning and fills in the values; the full app writes open-ended SQL with Claude.

    Search as
      Acme Robotics, its customers, orders and everyone in these documents are fictional. Orders are queried with SQLite compiled to WebAssembly (sql.js). Search runs on two small open models (all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2), downloaded once — about 50 MB — and cached by your browser.
