# Acme Robotics Product Catalog

Acme Robotics builds Pallet-runner autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) that move pallets and totes inside warehouses without floor markings. Every model ships with the Fleet Console software licence for its first year.

## PR-200 Tote Runner

The PR-200 carries totes and cases up to 200 kg at 1.5 m/s. It is built for pick-to-station workflows in e-commerce fulfilment. List price is $38,000 per unit. Standard lead time is 10 business days from order confirmation.

## PR-400 Pallet Runner

The PR-400 lifts standard pallets up to 400 kg and runs for 10 hours on a charge. It is the most common model for distribution centres moving full pallets between receiving, storage and dispatch. List price is $64,000 per unit. Standard lead time is 14 business days.

## PR-900 Heavy Runner

The PR-900 moves loads up to 900 kg, including double-stacked pallets and engine blocks, and is rated for cold storage down to minus 25 °C. List price is $112,000 per unit. Standard lead time is 21 business days, because each unit is load-tested before dispatch.

## Fleet Console

Fleet Console schedules missions, balances charging and shows every robot's position on the warehouse map. The first year is included; after that it costs $1,200 per robot per year.
