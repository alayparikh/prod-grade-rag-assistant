# Returns and Warranty

## Returns

Customers can return undamaged robots within 30 days of delivery for a refund minus a 15% restocking fee. Returns need a return authorisation (RMA) number from support before anything is shipped back. Returned orders are given the status returned once the robots arrive back at Acme.

## Warranty

Every Pallet-runner is covered for 24 months from delivery against defects in parts and workmanship, including the battery. Wheels and bumpers are wear parts and are covered for 12 months. The warranty does not cover damage from collisions with forklifts or from running outside the rated temperature range.

## Repairs

Warranty repairs are done on site within 5 business days in the West, Central and East regions, and within 10 business days in the EU. Out-of-warranty repairs are quoted before any work starts.
